Resource Profile: DDCC Practitioner

Official URL: http://smart.who.int/ddcc/StructureDefinition/DDCCPractitioner Version: 1.0.0
Draft as of 2025-09-16 Computable Name: DDCCPractitioner

Container for an DDCC Practitioner, which is a Practitioner authorized by the PHA to vaccinate an DDCC Patient.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: DDCC Immunization and DDCC Practitioner Role
  • CapabilityStatements using this Profile: WHO DDCC Registry Service

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics
