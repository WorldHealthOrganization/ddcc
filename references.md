## References

This implementation guide is meant to facilitate operationalization of the Digital Documentation of COVID-19 Certificates (DDCC) technical specifications and implementation guidance issued by the World Health Organization (WHO). This page includes links to the published DDCC: Vaccination Status (DDCC:VS) and DDCC: Test Result (DDCC:TR) guidance, as well as a small subset of other references from the guidance.

For additional resources, see the [Dependencies](dependencies.md) page, which includes the standards referenced in this implementation guide, the [Content Profiles](content_profiles.md) page for examples of DDCC implementations and use, and the references sections in the linked guidance documents.
